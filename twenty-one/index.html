TRUMP CARDS

— — ✦ — —
Shield+
The bet decreases by 2 while this card is on the table.
Shield
The bet decreases by 1 while this card is on the table.
Return
Remove the last card you drew to the deck; if there is one card left, nothing happens.
Reincarnation
Remove the last trump card your opponent places on the table. Draw 1 trump card.
Refresh
All your cards are replaced with 2 new ones.
Perfect Draw
Draw the best possible card from the deck.
One-up
The bet increases by 1 while this card is on the table.
Two-up
The bet increases by 2 while this card is on the table.
Hush
Draw a random face-down card. Enemy will not see the number on it.
Go for 17
The closest to 17 wins while this card is on the table. Replaces other Go For cards that are already on the table.
Go for 24
The closest to 24 wins while this card is on the table. Replaces other Go For cards that are already on the table.
Go for 27
The closest to 27 wins while this card is on the table. Replaces other Go For cards that are already on the table.
Friendship
Both players draw 2 trump cards.
Exchange
Swap the last cards drawn by you and your opponent. If there is only one card left, you cannot exchange it.
Destroy
Remove the last trump card your opponent places on the table.
Bloodshed
Draw 1 trump card. The bet increases by 1 while this card is on the table.
Bless
While this card is on the table, no one will die at the end of the round. If the trump card saved someone's life, the bet in the next round will be reduced by 1.
2-Card
Draws the 2 card; if this card is already on the table, nothing happens.
3-Card
Draws the 3 card; if this card is already on the table, nothing happens.
4-Card
Draws the 4 card; if this card is already on the table, nothing happens.
5-Card
Draws the 5 card; if this card is already on the table, nothing happens.
6-Card
Draws the 6 card; if this card is already on the table, nothing happens.
7-Card
Draws the 7 card; if this card is already on the table, nothing happens.
Disservice
Your opponent draws 1 card.
Remove
Remove the last card your opponent drew to the deck; if there is one card left, nothing happens.
